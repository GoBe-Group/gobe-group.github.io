Privacy Policy

Last updated · 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hamed Bakayoko, an individual sole trader (trading as “GoBe,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) of 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom, collects through the GoBe mobile app (the “App”), how we use and store it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. We are the data controller for your personal data and are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration number C1991885. This is a privacy notice, not a contract. Creating an account confirms that you were shown this notice; where we rely on consent, we ask for that consent separately.

GoBe is a messaging app. You talk to the people you have added, one to one or in groups, and a group chat can become a small app for whatever the group is sorting out (a vote, a rota, who's bringing what) by asking GoBe for one. You can also make a page about yourself that other people on GoBe can visit.

Earlier versions of GoBe were built around a map. The current version of GoBe does not use your location, your phone's motion sensors or Bluetooth, and does not ask for access to them. If you used an earlier version, section 7 explains what happens to what you made with it.

2. Account & Sign-In Information

GoBe offers two ways to create and sign in to your account.

  • Sign in with Apple — Apple shares your name and email address with us, including the private, randomly generated “relay” email Apple provides if you choose to hide your real email address.
  • Email and username sign-up — we collect the email address you give us (confirmed with a one-time code sent to it), and the username and password you choose. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored in plain text.

We use this information to create your GoBe account, to sign you in, and to identify you across sessions. Sign-in and sessions are handled by Supabase, which issues and stores the access and refresh tokens that keep you signed in.

3. Profile Information & Your Page

When you set up your profile, we collect the display name and username you choose, your date of birth (you must be at least 16), your phone number, and, optionally, a profile picture you choose or take with the camera.

Your phone number. Setting up an account requires a phone number. We store it in international format and use it to secure and recover your account, to contact you about your account where email is not enough, to help prevent one person from creating many accounts, and to let people who already have your number find you (section 6). It is never shown on your profile, never returned to another user by search or any other feature, and never shared with advertisers or data brokers. It is held separately from the rest of your profile, readable only by your own account. We confirm it by texting you a one-time code when you add it, so that a number on an account belongs to the person using it; a number can be confirmed on only one GoBe account. The code is sent by our provider Prelude (see section 11). We do not use it for marketing. It is deleted with your account.

Being found. Other signed-in users can search for people by display name or username. Search results show only your display name, username, profile picture and profile line. Anyone you have blocked, and anyone who has blocked you, is left out of your results and you out of theirs.

Your page. You can make a page about yourself: text, photos, links, songs and the other sections the App offers, in a look you choose, and optionally with a guestbook, polls and an ask box. Your page can be opened by any signed-in GoBe user except across a block, including by someone who follows a link to it. When someone signs your guestbook, votes in one of your polls or asks you a question, we store what they wrote or chose, who they are and when. A guestbook entry is shown on your page with the name of whoever wrote it. A question appears on your page only once you answer it, and nobody but you ever sees who asked it. A visit is counted once a day for each visitor: we record which account opened your page on which day so that the count is honest, and you see only the number. Photos on your page are stored in your own folder of our private photo storage.

4. Messages & Group Chats

You can message someone once you have both added each other, start a group with people you have added, and talk in the chat of a community or event you joined in an earlier version of GoBe.

What we store. What you write, photos you send, replies, reactions, edits and deletions, who sent each message, which chat it is in, when it was sent and how far each person has read. A group's name, emoji, look and its members, including who runs it (its admins), are stored too. Photos you send are kept in private storage that only the people in that chat can open.

Link previews. When you write a link to a web page in a message, the App opens that page from your phone to make a preview of it: the page's title, a line about it, the site's name and a small copy of its picture. The site, and wherever that picture is kept, sees a visit from your phone as it would if you opened the page yourself, including your IP address, but no cookies and nothing about your GoBe account. The preview is sent and stored with your message, its picture in the chat's private photo storage, so the people in the chat see it without visiting the site. You can take a preview off before you send, and it is deleted with the message.

Who can read them. A direct chat can be read by the two of you. A group chat can be read by everyone in it, including people added later. Nobody else can read your messages, including other GoBe users. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: they are encrypted in transit and stored on our servers so they reach every phone you use. We do not read them unless someone in the chat reports one (section 7), or the law requires us to.

Read receipts. In a direct chat, GoBe shows when your message has been read. You can switch read receipts off in Settings, in which case you will not see others' either.

Leaving and deleting. If you leave a group, its chat closes to you. If you delete a chat, it goes from your side only; the other people in it keep their copy. If the only person running a group deletes their account, the group is handed to whoever has been in it longest. Every message you have sent is deleted with your account.

Suggestions worked out on your phone. GoBe can suggest turning a chat into an app when the messages look like a plan being made. That suggestion is worked out on your phone from the messages already on it; nothing is sent to us or anyone else to make it. You can switch suggestions off in Settings.

5. Group Apps & Requests to GoBe's AI

A chat can become a small app. Anyone in a chat can ask for one, or ask for a change to it or to how the chat looks, by starting a message with “@GoBe”. Apps and changes made this way are shown to the chat to look over, and a group votes on them before they go live.

What is sent to Anthropic. To build an app from a request, our servers send the following to Anthropic, PBC, which provides the AI model (Claude) that does the building: the words of the request; the group's name and how many people are in it; the app as it stands, if there is one; and how the chat looks. If the people who run the chat's app have chosen to let GoBe read recent messages (the “AI access to messages” setting in the app's settings, which is off unless they turn it on), the text of the chat's last 30 messages is sent as well, without the names of who sent them. If you add a chat exported from WhatsApp to make an app from, the App reads it on your phone and sends only the names of the people in it (as shown in the App, never phone numbers, and you can change them), counts worked out from it (such as how many messages each person sent), and a sample of its messages with who sent them and when, leaving out links, numbers and postcodes. The export itself never leaves your phone, and we delete what was sent once the app is made. Please only add a chat everyone in the group was part of. Nothing else is sent: not your phone number, email, contacts, photos, or any other chat. Small changes (a name, a colour, the order of things) and the ready-made apps are worked out by our own servers and are never sent to Anthropic.

How Anthropic handles it. Anthropic processes these requests as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to use them to train its models. It keeps them only for the limited period those terms allow, for example to detect misuse, and then deletes them.

What is stored. We keep the request, the app that was built, its versions and how the group voted, and everything people put into the app (votes, names on a list, answers and the like). An app and its contents can be seen by everyone in its chat. They are deleted with the chat. When you delete your account, your votes and entries in apps are deleted or no longer linked to you; an app you asked for stays with its group.

Please do not ask GoBe to put anything in an app that you would not want the whole group to see, and remember that a request is read by an AI model.

6. Contacts & Finding People You Know

GoBe can help you find people you already know who are also on GoBe.

  • Being findable. Once your phone number has been confirmed by a code (section 3), people who already have that number in their contacts can find you on GoBe. A number that has not been confirmed is not used for this. This is on by default, and you can switch it off at any time in the App's Settings. For this, our servers keep an irreversible scrambled version of your number (a salted cryptographic hash), and that is what the matching uses: it never shows your number to anyone. Switching it off deletes that value.
  • Finding others. If you ask GoBe to check your contacts, the App asks for access to your address book and reads it on your device only. Your contacts are never uploaded. The App downloads a deliberately incomplete list of scrambled values, compares it against your contacts on the device, and sends back only the small number of entries that already appear to match, in scrambled form, so that we can tell you who they are. Contacts that do not match are never disclosed to us in any form.

We use this only to show you people you may know. We do not build a picture of people who are not GoBe users, we do not keep your address book, and we do not share any of it. Because being findable is on by default, we rely on our legitimate interest in helping people who already know each other find one another on GoBe; you can object at any time by switching it off.

7. Other Information We Keep

Adding people. When you add someone, we store the request; when they add you back, we store the connection between your two accounts so you can message each other. Either of you can remove it.

Invites. When you share an invite to a chat, the link carries a secret code. We store which chat it is for and who made it, and when someone joins with it, that they did. Whoever made it can withdraw it.

Reports and blocks. If you report a message, a page, a guestbook entry, a question or a person, we keep a record of the report: your account, a copy of what you reported and who wrote it, the reason you chose, and when. We keep the copy so the report can still be reviewed if the content is deleted afterwards. We use it to review the report and to meet our duties under the Online Safety Act 2023. Reports are visible only to us. If you block someone, we store your block list so that they cannot message you, see your page or find you. Your block list is private, and the people you block are not told. You can unblock people in Settings.

GoBe Plus. If you subscribe to GoBe Plus, Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. We receive and store Apple's record of the purchase (which plan, its transaction identifiers, when it started and when it renews or ends) so that GoBe knows your plan, and we keep a count of the apps your groups have asked GoBe to build so that the limits of your plan can be applied.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store your phone's push token so that we can tell you about new messages and about people adding you. You can mute any chat, and switch notifications off in Settings or in iOS Settings.

What you made with an earlier version. If you used an earlier version of GoBe, the stashes, trails, communities and events you made with it are still stored with your account, as described in the version of this policy in force when you made them, and are deleted with your account. The current version of GoBe does not show them on a map or collect anything new of that kind. You can ask us for a copy of them, or for them to be deleted, at any time (section 13).

8. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to: provide GoBe's features (messages, groups, group apps, pages and finding people you know); create and secure your account; sign you in across devices and sessions; show your profile, page and messages to the people they are meant for; moderate content and keep the service safe; apply your GoBe Plus plan; keep the App reliable and improve it; respond to support requests; and meet legal obligations.

Notifications. If notifications are on, we tell you about new messages in your chats (each chat can be muted) and when someone asks to add you or adds you back. The current version of GoBe does not send reminders to go anywhere or do anything, and does not send marketing by notification. The App's icon shows how many messages you have not read.

Email. We use the email address on your account for service email, which we send because you have an account: confirming your address, resetting your password, security notices, a reply when you contact us, and notice of a material change to this policy or our Terms. Earlier versions let people choose to receive occasional marketing email. The App no longer offers that, and anyone who chose it can stop it with the one-click unsubscribe link in any such email, which works without signing in. We keep a dated record of that choice and every change to it.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not use your messages, pages or app contents for advertising.

9. Our Legal Bases for Using Your Data

Where the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or a similar law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Contract — to create and run your account and provide the features you use, including sending your messages and building the apps your groups ask for.
  • Consent — for device permissions (notifications, contacts, camera and photos) and, where it was given in an earlier version, marketing email. Marketing email is sent only with your consent under regulation 22 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003. You can withdraw consent in the App, in iOS Settings, or through the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, without affecting earlier lawful processing, although the related feature may stop working.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure and improve GoBe, prevent abuse, understand which features are used, fix crashes, let people who know each other find one another, and moderate content, after balancing those interests against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with privacy, safety, consumer, and other applicable laws and lawful requests.
  • Vital interests or public interest — only in the exceptional circumstances in which applicable law permits and the basis genuinely applies.

Where another privacy law uses different grounds, we process information only for purposes permitted by that law.

10. Where Your Information Is Stored

GoBe's backend runs on Supabase. Your account, profile, page, messages, groups and group apps are stored in a Supabase Postgres database, and photos (profile pictures, photos on pages and photos sent in chats) in Supabase Storage. Our Supabase project is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt).

Photo storage is private: a photo cannot be opened by someone simply because they have guessed or obtained a link to it. Access is checked against who you are each time, so a photo sent in a chat opens only for the people in that chat.

11. Third Parties & Sub-Processors

We share information with a limited number of service providers who help us run GoBe:

  • Apple — provides Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, and App Store purchases of GoBe Plus.
  • Supabase — provides our database, file storage, server functions and sign-in, and stores the data described in this policy on our behalf, hosted in the EU.
  • Prelude — sends the one-time codes that confirm your phone number, by text message. Prelude receives your phone number, the code, and technical signals used to block fraudulent requests (such as your IP address), and nothing else about your account.
  • Stripe — takes payment when you buy GoBe Plus on our website instead of in the App. Stripe collects your card details itself, and we never see them. We give Stripe your email address and your account's internal ID so the subscription is linked to your account, and Stripe tells us whether it is active.
  • Anthropic — provides the AI model that builds group apps from “@GoBe” requests, as described in section 5. Anthropic is based in the United States.
  • PostHog — provides usage statistics and crash reports, hosted in the EU. We record a small, fixed set of app events (for example that a message was sent or a group app was made, never what it said) and, if the App crashes, where in the code it happened. These events are linked to your account's internal ID so that we can tell how many people use a feature and fix problems that affect particular accounts. They never include your name, email, phone number, contacts, messages, photos or anything you type. We do not record your screen. Your analytics history is deleted from PostHog when you delete your account.
  • Cloudflare — provides DNS, content delivery and security for our website. When you visit it, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request information to serve the page and block abusive traffic. It has no access to your GoBe account.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of GoBe or its users, or in connection with a sale of the business, in which case we will make reasonable efforts to notify you.

12. Data Retention

We keep your account, profile, page, messages and the rest of the information described here for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, which you can do at any time in Settings, we delete your account and everything linked to it: your profile, your page and everything on it, every message and photo you have sent, your votes and entries in group apps (or, where they belong to a group, their link to you), your connections, your block list and your analytics history. Some of this is deleted at once and the rest within 30 days. Groups you were in carry on without you. We keep limited records longer only where we must for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes, such as a report under review, and only for as long as that requires.

Apple keeps its own record of any GoBe Plus purchase, which you manage with Apple. Cancelling GoBe Plus does not delete your account, and deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your Apple account settings.

14. Children & Users Under 18

GoBe is not for anyone under 16. We use the date of birth entered at sign-up to enforce that rule and do not permit an account to be created when the stated age is under 16. If we discover that we collected information from an under-16 user, we will close the account and delete the information unless law requires limited retention.

For users aged 16 or 17, we apply high-privacy defaults and minimise collection. In the UK we take account of the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code. In the United States, GoBe is a general-audience service and is not directed to children under 13; if we gain actual knowledge that we collected a child's information, we will delete it and take the action required by COPPA. Parents or guardians may contact contact@gobeapp.co.uk.
