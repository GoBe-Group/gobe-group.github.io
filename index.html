02/07

Tear open what people left

sealed till you walk up to it

Get close and the foil comes off. Inside is a trace: a line, a photo or a film someone left on that exact spot. A second-hand shop with everything under a tenner. The bench that gets the last of the sun.

Like it, reply, or retrace it onto your own map. Then leave one of your own for whoever comes along next.

03/07

Uncover treats near you

free museums, gigs, deals & more

Some of what's sealed out there is for you to use: a free pastry with your coffee, a museum that costs nothing on a Sunday, a gig round the corner, a deal in a shop you walk past every day.

Walk up, unwrap it, and collect it at the door.

04/07

Keep everything you find

every sticker, note & photo

Everything you open goes into your storage. Traces you kept, and a sticker from every kind of place you've been: the bakery, the park, the planetarium, the gig.

It fills up the more you go out. Nobody can buy a sticker. You have to have been there.

05/07

Gift your finds to friends

up to ten things in one bag

Pick things from your storage, a trace, a sticker, an offer, and put them in a gift bag. Seal it and send it to a friend.

Not on GoBe yet? Send the gift by text. It waits for them, still sealed, for thirty days.

06/07

Cross paths, swap gifts

pass someone out walking: you each get one

Out on a walk at the same time as someone, a street apart? You've crossed. You each find the other in your drawer, face and name and nothing else, and a gift to open.

It's off until you turn it on, it only happens while you're walking, and GoBe never says where or when.

07/07

Make your page yours

deck it out with things you find

Your page is a scrapbook of where you've been. Pick a look, pin a trace you love, add GIFs, and stick on the stickers you found.

Every page is different, because nobody walked your walks.

Privacy

Your exact location stays off the map.

A map of real places has to keep people safe at them. Here's how GoBe does it.

Traces are saved to a rough spot on a grid about thirty metres across. The exact point is never stored, on your phone or on our servers.

Your walks stay private to you. Crossing paths is off until you turn it on, and works from a block about a hundred metres across, never a point.

Mark home, work or anywhere else as protected and GoBe keeps traces out of it and goes quiet inside it. Protected areas stay on your phone.

GoBe is free, carries no ads and never sells your data. The detail is in the Privacy Policy.

For investors, partners and press

The short version.

Social networks got very good at keeping people on their phones. GoBe is built to get them out of the house. You post by standing somewhere, what you can open depends on where you are, and the app is working when somebody goes out.

The map is the moat. It's filled in street by street by the people who walk them, it can't be copied into another city, and every trace, treat and find makes it more worth walking for everyone nearby. GoBe grows the way a place does, one street at a time.
