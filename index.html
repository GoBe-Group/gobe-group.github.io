How it works

Walk. Leave a trace. Go and find one.

That's the whole app. Everything you see on the map was left by someone who stood there.

Press play and walk

GoBe draws your route as a trail and the fog lifts behind you. The ground you clear stays clear all day.

Leave a trace

Write a line, snap a photo or film something. Your trace sits on the spot for whoever comes along next.

Go there to read it

Traces further off glow under the fog. Walk up to one and it opens. You have to turn up.

Trails

Press it again to keep it.

A trail records your route while you walk, for up to twelve hours. Press stop and you keep the lot: the line you walked, how far you went, how long you were outside and every trace you left on the way.

Switch location off halfway and GoBe bins the trail. A trail is a real walk, start to finish.

Traces

Leave a trace where you stood.

Write something: a tip, a thought, whatever's going on here. Or snap a photo. Whoever gets to that spot can read it, like it, comment and retrace it onto their own map.

The more people like it, the further it reaches. Your street, written by the people on it.

Sealed

Anything past a short walk stays sealed.

On the map it glows under the fog in its own colour: blue for a trace, yellow for a place, green for a community. In the drawer it comes wrapped in foil.

Tap it and GoBe tells you how many minutes away it is. Walk there and it opens.

Who left it and what it says: that's what the walk is for.

Who's around

You meet the people who walk your streets.

Everyone whose traces you can read has stood where you're standing. The streets decide who you run into.

What's around you

Everything round here lives on the map.

Places to eat, drink, read, train and shop sit between the traces. Communities and events are pinned where they meet.

Say you're going, turn up, and leave a trace while you're there.

Your ground

Tap it and see how you rank.

Every trail and trace counts towards your neighbourhood, your borough and your city. The board shows who's around and who has left the most.

Get out more, climb higher. The map of a place is made by the people who walk it.

Privacy

Your exact location stays off the map.

A map of real places has to keep people safe at them. Here's how GoBe does it.

Traces are saved to a rough spot on a grid about thirty metres across. The exact point is never stored, on your phone or on our servers.

Trails are more precise, so trails stay private to you.

Mark home, work or anywhere else as protected and GoBe keeps traces out of it. Protected areas stay on your phone.

GoBe is free, carries no ads and never sells your data. The detail is in the Privacy Policy.

For investors, partners and press

The short version.

Social networks got very good at keeping people on their phones. GoBe is built to get them out of the house. You post by standing somewhere, you see what's near you, and the app is working when somebody goes out.

The map is the moat. It's filled in street by street by the people who walk them, it can't be copied into another city, and every trace makes it more useful to everyone nearby. GoBe grows the way a place does, one street at a time.
